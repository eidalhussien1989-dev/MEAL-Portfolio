# Eid Al-Hussain | Senior MEAL & Field Research Specialist

## Professional Overview
Experienced Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability, and Learning (MEAL) Officer and Field Data Specialist with over 5 years of hands-on experience in humanitarian and research operations across Syria (Northeast Syria, Damascus, Daraa, Hasakeh, Deir ez-Zor, Raqqa). Proven leadership in managing large-scale survey teams, field data collection, baseline assessments, and data analytics.

* **Large-Scale Team Management & Survey Operations:** Led and supervised field data collection teams (managing up to 25 enumerators across 30 target villages in daily field setups, with extensive experience managing field networks covering 200+ villages and 225 enumerators for district-wide assessments).

* **Multi-Organization Experience:** Successfully executed field monitoring, evaluation, and research roles with leading international and local entities, including REACH Initiative / ACTED, Bahar Organization, Blumont, and the International Rescue Committee (IRC).
